Last updated: November 28, 2026

This policy explains what data the "Elhaqania Legal Consultation" website (the office of lawyer Mohamed Salah, Alexandria, Egypt) collects, why, how we protect it, and how to ask for deletion.

We use the data to run your account, verify payments, deliver files you bought, manage the community and prevent abuse, and answer your questions. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Data is stored in an access-controlled database. Payment images are kept in private storage and never shown publicly. We keep data while your account exists, and payment images for 90 days after the payment is settled, then delete them.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your account and data. Send the request from the account email to legalelhaqania@gmail.com or WhatsApp 01554797398. We will delete your account and related data within 30 days. We may keep what the law requires or what proves a settled payment.
